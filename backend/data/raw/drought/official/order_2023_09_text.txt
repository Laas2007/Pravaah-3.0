Proceedings of Karnataka Government
Subject: Declaration of drought hitten taluks in monsoon
season in the State for the year 2023-24.
Read: 1. Letter number: KSNDM C/2023/4/MISC/ 93, date:
22.8.2023 and 4.9.2023 of the Director, Karnataka State
Natural Disaster Monitoring Centre, Bengaluru.
2. Meeting proceedings of Cabinet Sub Committee held
on 18.7.2023, 22.8.2023, 4.9.2023 and 13.9.2023.
3. Circular No. RD/429/TNR/2023, date:25.8 2023 and
4.9.2023
4. Cabinet Note No. C.450/ 2023, date: 7.9.2023
Preamble:
The south west monsoon of the year 2023 has entered the State on
8.6.2023 and covered the entire state within 14 days, the entry and covering
of monsoon is delayed by one week than the usual onset date. In June month,
monsoon has been weakened and received 55% shortage of rainfall than usual
rainfall. In the month of July, monsoon has increased and 29% rainfall has
received, but this rain centralised only for a week. 73% rain has received
during the month of August, it is found that very less rain has recorded since
from the last 120 years. The State Weather Analysis Committee has examined
about the rain, crop, reservoir levels, underground water, drinking water and
situation of fodder from time to time and the Chief Secretary to Government
i.e; the Chairman of State Executive Committee of State Disaster Management
Authority has analysed.
As per the Drought Handbook 2020 of the Central Government and
based on the continued management of consequential indicators, and
according to the report of the Karnataka State Natural Disaster Monitoring
Centre from 1.6.2023 to 19.8.2023, the State has received 487 mm rainfall
and 23% deficit rainfall has received against the usual rainfall (635
mm.) As per the criteria indicated in guidelines of Drought Handbook- 2020,
after examining i.e; deficit of rainfall more than 60%, dry atmosphere in 3
continuous weeks, shortage of humidity, and consequential criteria that is
shortage of humidity, satellite based crop indicator and crop sowing area and
severity in water resource indicator drought condition has found in total 113
taluks.
In the subcommittee meeting held on 22.8.2023, the Deputy
Commissioner has formed teams comprising agriculture, horticulture and
revenue officers/other staff of respective district and randomly identified 1519
villages in total 113 taluks and taken up Ground Truthing through mobile
app in 14228 villages of 113 taluks. Accordingly, a circular has been issued
Page 7
in (3) read at above on 25.08.2023 and instructed to take up crop survey.
Accordingly, 62 taluks in which more than 50% crop loss has occurred and
51 taluks where 33% to 50% crop loss has occurred have categorised as severe
drought and normal drought, respectively.
During the period of 1.6.2023 to 2.9.2023, 512 mm rainfall has received
and 27% deficit rainfall has received when comparing to 701 mm of usual
rainfall. By considering the drought condition, KSNDMC has given report that
draught condition has occurred in 83 Taluks, newly. In the cabinet
subcommittee meeting held on 4.9.2023, it has been decided to take up
Ground Truthing in 134 taluks including 83 taluks in the State as per the
report of 2.9.23 along with 51 taluks identified as common drought condition
by conducting crop loss survey, already and submit the report to the Cabinet
about the said proposal. Accordingly, in the circular read at (3) above dated
4.9.2023, it has been in instructed to carry out the crop survey.
In the Cabinet meeting held on 7.9.2023 read at (4) above, the Cabinet
has discussed in detail about the drought condition occurred due to deficit of
rainfall in south west monsoon in the state. Already it is confirmed by the
crop loss survey that severe drought has occurred in 62 taluks and
additionally the Deputy Commissioner should form teams comprising of
agriculture, horticulture and revenue officers/other staff of respective
districts and identify randomly out of total 134 Taluks and take up ground
truthing in total 19,075 plots through mobile app and since the report will be
received in few days, the Cabinet has decided to determine about the drought
declaration, later on.
In the Cabinet Subcommittee meeting held on 3.9.2023, a report about
crop loss survey has been obtained from the Deputy Commissioner for the
134 taluks, additionally and after taking up ground truthing once again in 51
identified taluks, 41 taluks have identified in severe drought category and the
remaining 10 taluks have identified under common drought category. Out of
83 taluks in which draught condition has occurred newly, 58 taluks have
been categorised under severely drought (large deficit) and 24 taluks as
common drought. Accordingly, as per the guidelines of drought declaration
given in drought management handbook- 2020 of the Central Government,
considering the criteria of drought declaration, out of 236 taluks of 31
districts of the state, drought condition has occurred in 195 taluks. It has
been decided to obtain the approval of the honourable Chief Minister and
Chairman of Karnataka State Disaster Management Authority, since the
emergency drought management responsibility of declaring 161 taluks as
large deficit taluks and 34 taluks as common deficit taluks, after conducting
Ground Truthing in all taluks finally. All these processes have been brought
to the notice of the Chairman of the Executive Committee of KSDMA, i.e; the
Chief Secretary to the Government, from time to time.
Page 8
As per the decision of the Cabinet subcommittee of 13.09.2023, the
Government has obtained the approval of the Honourable Chief Minister and
out of 236 taluks of 31 districts of the State, drought condition has occurred
in 195 taluks and after taking up Ground Truthing in all these taluks, finally
decided to declare 161 taluks as severely drought hitten (large deficit) taluks
and 34 taluks as common drought hitten (common deficit) taluks.
Accordingly, the following order has been issued.
GOVERNMENT NO. RD 449 TNR 2023,
Date: 13.09.2023.
In the backdrop of the facts explained in the proposal, as per the
drought management hand book-2020 of the Central Government and as per
the Ground Truthing report of the Deputy Commissioner, drought condition
has occurred in 195 taluks out of 236 taluks of 31 districts of the State during
the monsoon season of the year 2023. Out of which, it is ordered by declaring
161 taluks as severely drought hitten (large deficit) taluks (Annexure-1) and
34 taluks as common drought hitten (Annexure-2) taluks with immediately
effect up to next 6 months’ period or till the further order, whichever is earlier.
The guidelines as per SDRF/NDRF rules will be given by the
Government from time to time, so as the Deputy Commissioner could take up
the programmes of drought management.
By order and in the name of the
Governor of Karnataka,
Sd/-
(T.C.KANTHARAJ)
